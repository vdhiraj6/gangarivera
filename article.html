PROPERTY BUYER'S GUIDE · BRIJ GHAT

Buying a residential plot near Brij Ghat? Start with these checks.

A plot can look right in a photograph and still need careful checking on paper. If you are exploring residential plots around Brij Ghat and Garhmukteshwar, take your time, visit the site and get clear answers before paying a booking amount.

1. Visit the actual site

Do not rely only on a brochure, a map screenshot or a video. Visit the location in person. Check the approach road, entry, surrounding area, boundary and the condition of the internal roads. If possible, visit during the day and take your own photographs.

2. Match the plot with the latest layout

Ask for the latest signed layout and identify the exact plot number, dimensions, area, road width and direction. The Ganga Rivera material shared for this page shows multiple plot sizes, including 100, 120 and 200 sq. yd. options. Confirm the currently available inventory and dimensions in writing; a brochure is not a substitute for the latest allotment details.

3. Ask for the complete price breakup

Compare the basic rate with the full amount payable. Ask the seller to list applicable development charges, club or membership charges, location or corner premiums, taxes, stamp duty, registration expenses, utility or meter charges and any maintenance amount. Get the payment schedule and refund or cancellation terms in writing before making a payment.

4. Have the property documents independently checked

Ask for the land title chain, ownership records, land-use details, approved or sanctioned layout, applicable permissions, encumbrance information and the draft agreement or allotment terms. Confirm whether RERA registration applies and, if it does, verify the registration details with the relevant authority. Consult an independent property lawyer before signing or paying.

5. Separate completed facilities from proposed amenities

Project presentations may show a clubhouse, pool, parks, gym, temple, security and other facilities as part of the concept. Ask which facilities are already completed, which are under development and which are proposed. Check the written specifications and timelines rather than assuming every visual shown in a brochure is already available.

6. Check the route yourself
