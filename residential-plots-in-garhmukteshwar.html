How to compare residential plots

  • Location: Visit the site and verify the approach road, nearby services and the exact plot pin.
  • Documents: Independently check ownership, title chain, land use, encumbrances and applicable approvals.
  • Layout: Match plot dimensions and numbers with the latest signed or approved layout.
  • Infrastructure: Confirm the present status of roads, electricity, water, drainage and security arrangements.
  • Total cost: Include basic price, PLC, development charges, taxes, meter charges, stamp duty and registration.
  • Payment terms: Get the payment schedule, cancellation terms and possession conditions in writing.
